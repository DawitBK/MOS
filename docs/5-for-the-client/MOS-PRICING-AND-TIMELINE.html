1. Summary

  • One fixed price: ETB 986,615. It is 98.7% of the ETB 1,000,000 cap, leaving ETB 13,385 unused.
  • What it buys: all six modules (procurement with supplier payment, inventory, lab and quality, gate and weighbridge, production, sales with receivables), every given requirement, and everything needed to go live.
  • When:
WeeksWhat happens
1–20The system is built in ten two-week sprints. Each module goes to your key users as soon as it is built
by 4 · by 16Test server on your infrastructure · production server ready
21–22System test and final acceptance test
23Training and the migration rehearsal
24Go-live
25–26Close support (hypercare)
27–32The remaining standard and full features (the v1.1 completion), unless built earlier
  • How the price is split: by the share of the estimated work in each feature, sprint, phase and module. Every figure below comes from the same 4,027 hours of estimated work (3,177 hours of building and 850 hours of go-live work), at ETB 245 per hour of estimated work.
  • You pay as work is delivered (§8): 10% at signature, then the value of what is accepted at each review.

Words used here

WordMeaning
Estimated workThe hours a feature should take, at the likely estimate. The price follows it, so a feature's price shows its size
Core / standard / fullDepth of a feature. Core is the flow with its key controls, delivered by go-live; standard and full are year-one conveniences and extras, delivered in the release if time allows, otherwise in the v1.1 completion (weeks 27–32). All three are in the price
SprintA two-week step of work, ending with a review where you see what was built
UATUser acceptance testing: your key users test the system against agreed scripts
HypercareClose daily support right after go-live

10. How the price was built

  1. The work: every given requirement was broken into 199 features and each was estimated in hours (implementation plan Appendix A): 2,439 hours of core, 738 of standard and full. The go-live services add 850 hours (servers, testing, migration, training, cutover, hypercare).
  2. The price: fixed at ETB 986,615, within the ETB 1,000,000 cap, and spread over the 4,027 hours of estimated work at ETB 245 each. The same rate is used everywhere, so every line of this document adds up to the total.
  3. The timeline: the recommended plan with three developers, AI assistance and a QA engineer (sprint plan). The price does not change with the team we use; the dates and the scope are what we commit to.
  4. Checks: the phases, modules, sprints, features and payments each add up to ETB 986,615 exactly (checked by script).
